Delete your AG Live account

Package: com.agfarmer.live

In the app

Open AG Live and sign in. Open Profile at the top and choose Account deletion. Confirm the dialog. The account and associated data are deleted in the app.

By email

Email ag.farmer.mail@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject “AG Live account deletion”. Include the email or phone on the account. We delete the account data within a reasonable period, except records required for legal or fraud prevention.

Privacy policy: https://www.ag-live.in/privacy-policy
