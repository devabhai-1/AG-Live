Delete your AG Live account

App: AG Live · Package: com.agfarmer.live · Developer: Apex Global Team LLC

Privacy Policy · Terms · Contact

In the app

  1. Open AG Live and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose Account deletion.
  4. Confirm the dialog. The account is deleted in the app. You do not leave the app to do this.

By email

Email ag.farmer.mail@gmail.com from the address on the account. Subject: “AG Live account deletion”. Include the email or phone number on the account. We delete the profile and associated chat data within a reasonable period. We may keep a short record if the law or fraud prevention requires it.
