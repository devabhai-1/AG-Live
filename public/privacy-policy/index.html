2. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, phone number, age (18+), and address, when you create a profile.
  • Farming questions: chat text you type, and crop photos you choose to send, so the app can suggest a treatment.
  • Camera: used only when you open a photo check or a video call. We do not record the camera in the background.
  • Microphone: used only while a voice or video call is open, so the assistant can hear your question. We do not listen in the background.
  • Optional Google sign-in: your Google account email and name, if you choose that login.
  • Technical logs: app errors and security logs needed to keep the service working.

3. How we use data

We use this data to answer farming questions, apply free and paid plan limits, keep chat history after you sign in, and reply to support. We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. AI answers and reporting

Chat, photo, voice, and video replies are generated by AI. They are farming guidance, not a lab test or a licensed agronomist. Inside the app, every AI reply has a Report button. You can flag offensive, wrong, or harmful content without leaving AG Live. Reports are sent to us for review.

5. Who else processes data

A request is sent to secure cloud and AI services only to produce that answer. Sign-in may use Google. Purchases made in the app are processed by Google Play. We do not sell personal information.

6. How long we keep data

We keep account and chat data while the account exists. When you delete the account, we delete the associated profile and chat data, except short records we must keep for fraud prevention or the law.

7. How to delete your account

In the app: sign in, open Profile, and choose Account deletion, then confirm. On the web: https://www.ag-live.in/account-deletion/. Or email ag.farmer.mail@gmail.com from the registered address with the subject “AG Live account deletion”.

8. Children

AG Live is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
