About AG Live

AG Live is a live farming assistant for chat, camera, and voice, so farmers can prevent crop disease and keep the crop healthy.

Crop limit

Poppy-crop help is only for disease prevention and healthy growth on a government-licensed field. Narcotic use, lancing, and latex are not part of this app.
